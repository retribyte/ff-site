# FF2 stories — copyedit review

Proposed fixes for the seven FF2 stories in `md/stories/` (source:
`wiki/sources/stories/FF2/Final Frontier 2 Stories.docx`). Each item is
`before` → `after`.

Status: **Tiers 1–2 applied and uploaded** (2026-10-02). From Tier 3, four
items are decided and applied:
- #1: Chomsky's `i` → `I` (64×).
- #2: `Daerthlorn`.
- #3: `Channel Eleven`.
- #4: `wandered`.

Tier 3 items #5–#10 were not answered and are left as written. Separately,
Airlocked! P1's `"You're not hurting him!"` was re-attributed from Sanya to
Hector. Also applied: the slur standard from
`REVIEW-slur-standardization.md`. Seth's `furfag` (2×, Airlocked! Part 1)
became `furbing`, using the short f-slur → `bing` mapping.

Tiers:
1. **Mechanical** — punctuation and typing artifacts. They don't change wording.
2. **Spelling / clear grammar errors**
3. **Author-voice calls** — these could be deliberate. Decide each one.
4. **Continuity flags** — not copyedits. Listed for awareness only.

Left alone on purpose: profanity and slurs, in-universe words (Squoat,
squather, squon, squabe, Grreat, Furgaz…), Sanya's hissing ("emotionsss"),
trailing-off dialogue ("m--", "Do--"), stylized ellipses in Chomsky's journal
("Um…..", "Unless….", "felt....something"), the deliberately repeated
"Step 1)" joke, "Kyle" for KYL (FF2 transcripts use it 156×), and British
"pummelled".

## Tier 1 — Mechanical

### A Beginning
Sanya's stories were typed on a keyboard that produced `¨` for quotes and `´` for apostrophes.
- every `¨…¨` → `"…"` (12 lines)
- `Seth´s`, `Sanya´s`, `can´t` → `Seth's`, `Sanya's`, `can't`
- `¨Robot?"` → `"Robot?"`
- `"There are plenty of emotions like that, you would have to be specific.¨ KYL thought some more, and then replied ¨Hmm. My best bet would be Dread. Dread.` → `"There are plenty of emotions like that, you would have to be specific." KYL thought some more, and then replied, "Hmm. My best bet would be Dread." Dread.` (adds the missing closing quote)

### A Conversation Between Gods
- `…in our *'little conversations'*?` → add the missing closing `”`

### The Interviews
- `"Not a word about this, Channel Six.”` → mismatched quote marks, make both straight
- `"No, no!", the purple Squoatling cried.` → `"No, no!" the purple Squoatling cried.`
- `"Yes, Channel 11?", Emmett exhaled.` → `"Yes, Channel 11?" Emmett exhaled.`

### Chomsky's Journal & Audio Logs

- `C: “Hmm. these are…` → `C: “Hmm. These are…`

## Tier 3 — Author-voice calls (decide each one)

1. **Chomsky's lowercase "i".** The journal uses `i` 64× and `I` 63×, often within the same sentence ("I however… i wasn't"). The options:
   - Leave it as Chomsky's voice.
   - Normalize everything to `I`.
   - Make it consistently lowercase.

   Since it's half-and-half, it reads as a typing habit rather than a deliberate voice.
2. **Planet name: Daerthlorn vs Dearthlorn.** Airlocked! has `Daerthlorn` and The Interviews has `Dearthlorn`. Across the wiki and archive sources it's 4 vs 3. Pick one.
3. **`Channel 11` vs `Channel Eleven`** in The Interviews. Make them consistent?
4. **`laundered back to his room`** (Airlocked! Finale). This is probably "wandered", but it could be "lumbered".
5. **`His experience was fading quickly`** (Airlocked! P2, adrift in space). This probably means his oxygen or consciousness. Rewrite or leave?
6. **`Although he had no collection of his own strength`** (Airlocked! Finale). This is probably "recollection" (he doesn't remember himself on The Crave). Change?
7. **`his romantic affliction with plants`** (Airlocked! Finale). This is either a deliberate pun or should be "affection for". Leave?
8. **`We just need to be home by nine in the afternoon`** (Zombification). This is odd for a real clock. It could be an in-universe quirk. Leave?
9. **`I think I found a burial grounds`** (Zombification, a kid speaking). Leave it as dialogue or change to "burial ground"?
10. **`No, I talked to him about you and I`** (Airlocked! Finale, dialogue). The grammatical form is "you and me", but it's fine as speech. Leave?
