After he lost his two best friends, Emmett was in bad shape. Sure, the two taught him the basics of how the Galaxy works, how others reproduce, and how to kill someone without reason, however he was still lost without them. He also swore vengeance at the Grasshoppers, the ones who nearly killed him and them.

After a week of remorse, Emmett set out to find the others: the dark elf who had a love affliction with Seth (she escaped his backpack somehow), the bisexual human who made a dog in his belly button, and the black Kirby who barely had a storyline. He gathered these people up in an attempt to create a new bounty hunting group, one that would be a reminder to him of his past adventures. However, the scene did not prevail; The Kirby did not want to do any dirty work, Jacob masturbated all day, and the dark elf constantly wanted to fraternize with Emmett. Quickly they went their separate ways, and Emmett was alone once more. With nothing left but his ship, the Vespoid travelled back home. To "Russia".

Upon his entry to his home planet, Emmett was greeted by many excited and curious faces. They inquired him with wonders of riches, exploration, and from some, conquest. However, Emmett mostly pushed their curiosity aside. He wanted some time alone, to figure out a way to find Seth and Math without being blasted to pieces.

Emmett's popularity went south quickly. He did not open himself to the others, which was irritating to the majority. They didn't know what was in his mind. The officials realized his stature as more and more complaints appeared about him, and they were not wanting their only space pilot to shut himself away. They sent him off to a variety of places to try and get him to relax and ooze his information out. It was only until he was at the spa and psychologist joint whenever he was able to.

The officials thought that the best way to take his mind off the matter would be to get him to work on-planet. Emmett earned himself a title of Labor Coordinator, a Vespoid job so tantalizing and detail-oriented that it was impossible for him to focus on the past. As a Vespoid by instinct enjoys mass coordination, Emmett essentially became a workaholic. Sure, the days were long and his breaks were short, but Emmett felt right at home, away from the complications of space.

After two years of hard work, the Vespoid was given the opportunity to coordinate a much larger ordeal than labor: interstellar space travel. He was to take the role of the Chairman of the Vespoid Armada due to his experience in space travel. He took this promotion hesitantly, due to his fear of what the space environment may have been at the time. The Vespoid fleet was now under his control, with him in the safe confines of home.

The first hundred missions he took control over were normally about resource gathering and trade. His empire wanted to grow and prosper under their dependence upon otherworldly materials. These missions were rarely inclusive of conflict; Emmett knew when he was dominant in a situation or not. Slowly, these missions sent the Vespoids from a state of early space exploration to the creation of a fully sustainable energy source for their planet. Their numbers grew, and so did Emmett's knowledge on the galaxy.

The next prominent missions were focused upon colonization and energy gathering. These missions each included on average a year's worth of terraforming, hard work, and stress on the Chairman's behalf. Very slowly did the Vespoids advance, from taking over their entire solar system to looming upon planets on others. During this time, Emmett was also accredited for his assistance in their creation of galactic weaponry, particularly the prototype of the Planetary Eradicator and the Hull Vaporizer. This period ended whenever the Vespoid fleet came upon and "accidentally" shot a warhead into the Grasshopper sector.

War was declared between the Vespoids and Grasshoppers once more. These years were most certainly the most hazardous to Emmett's health. They reminded him of his past, of Seth and Matthias, and sent him into a trance. There were many occasions where he lost men and ships due to his failed attempts at finding his old pals. For decades the two species were in a constant stalemate, capturing and losing star systems on a daily basis. This tie was broken upon the Vespoid introduction of the actual Planetary Eradicator, where a simple threat lay the Grasshoppers to his knees. Emmett, tiresome of this war, gave his people control over what to do with their adversaries, and was crowned the leader of the Vespoids as the late Flyshrinker (the last leader) was assassinated by one of his own people in the chaos of war.

(The Grasshoppers were promptly enslaved by the Vespoids by a vote. Other popular options were to take their resources and leave them to die, eradicate all of their owned colonies, or to make a treaty with them.)

As he aged into his 70's, Emmett was dying. Sure, he had lived a full and eventful life, however his people wanted and needed him as a leader. By the people's choice, he received multiple biomechanical surgeries, enhancing his life force and allowing him to escape death for another seventy years.

These surgeries nearly put the Vespoids in bankruptcy.

With renewed strength, the Leader decided to take a mercantile approach for his people. He started a trade company for a delicacy no one in the whole galaxy had aside from the Vespoids: LifeHoney™.

Advertising was horrendous at first. Without much money, Emmett had to take multiple loans from the Intergalactic Bank in order to get his industry started. He began with fifteen second adverts on holoposters and space billboards. As time continued he advanced his prospects to holo television, video games, and spaceship appliances. Soon, everyone wanted a piece of their savory nectar.

It took thirty years of trade, fighting off pirates, and diplomatic trade routes in order to keep the Vespoids financially stable. However, they were better off than before, and the Leader was celebrated once more. Again, Emmett was tiresome. Focusing upon the LifeHoney trade bored him. He allocated the task to another planet in his domain and went to task on other ordeals.

For the next forty years, Emmett retired. He chose a trustworthy and much younger heir for his monarchical spot and rested himself in a comfortable spot as an advisor to the Leader. Emmett was still appreciated for what he had done for his people, and they understood why he would step down from his position.

These last forty years flew by quickly. Emmett occasionally took to the stars once again in his relaxation, traveling to the unknown (at least, for him) and safe reaches of space. He was met by many women who knew of his efforts and or commercialisation, but he blew them off. He was far too old for him to be playing with them. These women and his space adventures reminded him once more of Seth and Matthias and the rest of the crew. Was he really relaxed, having no clue of where they were?

In the last twenty years of his life, Emmett went on a side mission to find them, to see their faces once more. There was nothing stopping him, aside from a stroke that affected him at a hundred forty-one. He sped to his home planet and died a year later there.

A hundred forty-two years of being affected by his life at age twenty.

Emmett's body rested in the dirt for a few days before a group of grave robbers stole him from his coffin. These men dragged him to a laboratory remote from the breaches of monarchical law and society itself. They wanted to build him anew. To somehow resurrect the dead and glorify him for what they thought he was: the best Vespoid ever.

Through the kidnapping a random Vespoid, they experimented, organically augmenting the living body and keeping Emmett's brain safe and functional. Their glorification tactic was to put him in a body that represented Emmett's symbol: a golden wasp. They nearly killed the random Vespoid's body in their efforts, having to inject gold and rip apart the stinger in the creation of a smaller, more comfortable one.

Their selfish deed was done whenever they implanted his brain into the chassis, electrifying the body in hopes to bring him back.

After a failed attempt, it worked. Emmett was back, in a golden wasp. His voice was then amended to sound more like his young, space faring self, his eyes were far better than the average Vespoid's, and he was overall an astounding work of art.

The government found out of the scientists' efforts quickly and put an end to it. They nearly killed Emmett before they realized it was him.

The officials knew it was dangerous for Emmett to introduce his status to the people. It would ruin Vespoid life as they knew it. They changed his name and shipped him off to a planet he personally conquered and made him a Leader there.

Although it has been sixty years since then, Emmett has not grown much at all. The scientists illegally injected a mixture of LifeHoney, Nanofibers, and Antibiotics in order to try and make him immortal. Their efforts were rewarded in that aspect, however they were not entirely successful. Emmett's body grows old at a very slowly rate instead of giving him immortality. (And the healing of wounds is the same rate as a Vespoid's. The mixture focuses on age, not wounds.)
