# Venus — character sheet (FF1)

Archived for wiki use. Cut from the episode 8 transcript as OOC; posted by
Mica (Discord), 20-Jan-18 10:38 PM, when she joined FF1 as Venus. Verbatim
from `md/ff1/raw/8.md` (typos kept).

---

Venus is a half human- half robot character, about 5'11 and 130 lbs. She is made out of a type of iindestructible titanium alloy. Her human features include about 50% of her face, upper body, and one leg. She is very flexible, and only uses a rail gun as her weapon of choice. She is secretive, quiet, and shy. She doesn't understand normal human customs, as her own planet is highly different from Earth. Venus escaped from her planet after she was taken from her sector by rogue sex traffickers. She does not like sexual acts forced upon her unless explained.

Age: 20 (human) 6.0 (android)
Personality: Takes everything literally. Hard to joke around with.
Colors: matte white, silver, and light pink led lights.
Attire: Holographic bodysuit, pink thigh boots.
Hair color: light blonde
