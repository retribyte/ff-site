# Chapter 1: Entry 1

*35-4, 3009 GUY · Translated from Squoatian to English*

After spending about a semester not fitting in with Seth's ragtag crew, I was sent to a private therapist for some reason. I think Seth thought it was a good idea for me to speak my heart out to some stranger regarding my home planet's destruction. Anyhow, I'm stuck with an assignment of writing at least a journal entry a week about my feelings and that stuff. I do not know what would happen if I did not turn in twenty-five entries for my next session, but I find this a good excuse to hide from Valdorris. He's a big bully and I don't feel that safe around him. Seth never shows that he cares about Valdorris being a jerk to me, at least not in my presence.

I don't really know how to feel about what's happened with Squoatopia. One night I'm resting in my bed, studying for a math test, and the moment I wake up, I'm pinned underneath some sandsteel sheets, and my room is in flames. Seth saved me from the wreckage with the most somber face. He let me look for my satchel and my worship doll before he grabbed me and managed to knock me out by accident on the way out. Why would the llamanians betray us like that? We've been friends with them for centuries and developed together as a pair of species. There's no way they would suddenly attack us like that. Unless Seth had something to do with it. I'll keep this posted with whatever information I get.

I really wish I met a llamanian. There's so much I missed by listening to my parents. They kept me inside at all times, only able to go out in the backyard for an hour a day. I hate to say it, but I don't really miss them. They always said they loved me very much but they never really showed it, only really taking care of me two days a week. They claimed they were always busy with taking care of the business, but how busy can managing a bakery be? Enough to warrant neglecting their only son? I've read online of others my age going out and basking in the sunlight with their parents, really spending quality time together. I just had dinner with them, had lousy conversations about my supposed importance, and got scolded for my occasional Q on a test (most of those test grades were so close to an S, the questions were just badly worded).

All I can think of is llamanians right now, and how kind and gentle they are. Well, how kind and gentle they were. Having to study their culture really opened my eyes to them, and well... It's really hard to believe they destroyed Squoatopia out of malice. I miss my home.

I've been mainly stuck on this ship since then, never really engaging on any missions with Seth and his crew. Seth is an okay caretaker, I guess. He doesn't really know how to interact with me, and I think he may be afraid of disappointing me. But he makes sure I'm well taken care of, even if it means the others have to cook dinner and wash clothes. I can do this myself, but he insists otherwise. I imagine it must be hard to take care of a kid when you've never had one yourself. Valdorris, Evensga, and HAG-2X don't share his affection towards me. Valdorris has a terrible attitude and only cares about himself. My mere presence makes him think I'm a waste of space and resources. He's joked about shooting me out the airlock while Seth wasn't looking once. Evensga is a very pretty woman, but every time I'm near her she avoids my gaze. She seems to speak just fine with the others, but I think she doesn't really like kids. She likes making out with Valdorris and Seth a LOT though, enough for Seth to kick me out of the room. HAG-2X is just a robot, and robots are just programmed to be helpful tools. They aren't really friend material.

Anyhow, I'm running out of space for this entry. See you next time.

EDIT: Seth got very angry when I asked him if he caused the llamanians to destroy the city, blaming everything on them. Weird.

# Chapter 2: Entry 348

*9-27, 3012 GUY · Translated from GU Binary to English*

## Part 1

Today was a mess. This whole week was a mess. I think it's a bit hard to write what just happened, so I'll explain it in steps, starting from Turkal. This may extend into another entry if it gets too long.

Turkal, Seth and the crew were in the middle of planning this huge heist that could get us out of debt. I was in the living block, overhearing the meeting. Upon trying to put in my two cents in the conversation, Valdorris immediately growled and glared at me, as if I was violating him from my presence alone. Honestly, I do not understand why he always hated me. I know he and Seth were close friends before I came in, but that does not excuse him to be an asshole to me for three whole GUYs. After I receded back to the living block, the two of them started arguing obnoxiously, really stressing me out.

Rezet, I found Seth and Evensga having sex in the living block after I took a shower. Of course, I didn't know what sex was at the time, as Seth managed to kick me out every time things started to head in that direction for him. Seth apparently expected me to stay in my quarters to work on an assignment regarding the plan the equinox before. Evensga practically yelled at me for being in the room, and Seth muttered vulgarities under his breath, ashamed. To make matters even worse, my body started to react to the situation in an embarrassing way, and I was dismissed. I stayed in my room the rest of the day and couldn't bear to see Seth.

Ever evening Seth talked to me about the ears and the caves, or sex. Things got really uncomfortable to say the least, especially when he was pointing at my crotch and stuff. Girls are kinda gross, at least the ones I've seen on missions with Seth. I don't really understand why someone would want to do... that to a person not of their species.

Rimbo was surprisingly peaceful. It was probably because the others were out at some bar where you have to be 18 GUYs old. That assignment Seth gave me was really stressful so... Needless to say, I felt pretty good at the end of the day, despite making a mess of my work.

Overt had the crew being ambushed by a llamanian private's ship. While the fight was over quickly, he nicked my neck, having mainly aimed for me after boarding our ship. I do not think I would have survived if HAG-2X hadn't tripped the llamanian. Seth babied me afterwards, bandaging up my neck and making me a cup of hot sweets. Valdorris, while he seemed relieved I was okay, did not approve Seth's parenting.

## Part 2

On Elena, Seth was really trying to push me getting a girlfriend. I think he still felt guilty about what happened on Rezet, but he really was making things worse. Valdorris kept on looking up GU child celebrities and trying to hook me up with them, obviously just being a jerk. I got to say that despite Valdorris being a jerk constantly to me, he always gets under my skin. I think because he was Seth's best friend I unconsciously compared myself to him or something significant like that. I don't know. Also Seth apparently spent most of our spending money on the upcoming adventure. I don't think that was a good idea.

Mono I was angry at Seth for spending all of our cash. Why would he even do that? The Siege of Moldarr could theoretically give us an infinite amount of Ducketts, as the Moldak technically produce the components for Ducketts with their fecal matter. Seth wanted to build a monopoly that could oppose the GU. I just don't understand why he would even try such a thing. Slavery is terrible and having all the money in the galaxy only leads to sadness. I learned both of these morals in Flirts in Esquow. I'm surprised I even remember that book. It just makes me feel lonely remembering that most of my childhood is gone now. Fourteen GUYs and wasting away on a carnival ship.

Eflo I finished the plans for the Siege of Moldarr. We were all supposed to paralyze colony after colony of Moldak with the signets Seth paid for. Once a Moldak leader accepted the contract the signet sealed, all members sharing his DNA structure in a huge radius would effectively be ours to control. I was supposed to act as a crew of political figures, with Seth being the president, Evensga being his wife, and HAG and Voldarris acting as bodyguards. I was supposed to be the ambassador handing out the contracts and making fake deals. This plan was really stressful and if it backfired, it was going to bite us in the ass. I don't think they really understood how paranoid the Moldak are of intrigue. They assured me that if things went tits up, they would just take the Moldak by force. The eventual outcome definitely proved otherwise. That night we celebrated and ate Brauflags (really good but gassy meal).

Today (Rinns) was the day of the siege. We all dressed up in dress suits and the others applied makeup to make themselves more presentable. They offered to give me a makeover but I don't really like stuff on my face. I find it difficult to get it out once it is on. We traveled to a few colonies and the mission was mostly successful. There were only four more colonies to take over and spirits were high. That was until my stomach started to feel terrible. In the middle of meeting with the next colony's leader, I desperately needed to use the restroom and asked for directions to one. Unfortunately, my stomach problems ended up getting our group caught and detained while I was out doing my business. I came back to see Seth restrained by a rather buff security guard and the rest of the crew cornered to a wall. They mentioned that they knew how infamous our crew was and that if they bagged Seth, they would get the resources to make their colony the head of the planet. They told me to join the others and let my captain be killed, but I could not let that happen. I hate the idea of killing someone, but in the spur of the moment, I shot the Moldak holding Seth and grabbed Seth's arm to run towards the others. However, the Moldak cornering them managed to impale them with their Pummel-Spears.

## Part 3

Got to admit that I was in tears. Before I could break down, Seth grabbed me and fled to the ship, taking out the Moldak trying to rob us. Seth slammed me down in the captain's seat and told me what buttons to press to get off the planet. We escaped the Moldak without much trouble and Seth turned on the autopilot.

My heart was pounding, a slew of emotions reigning over me. We just lost people we've been with for a very long time all because of a stupid plan to get unfathomably rich. There was a bit of silence in the ship before Seth told me that he's happy I am alive and safe. I feel guilty, as my actions practically condemned the others to death, three lives to one. Seth doesn't really care, telling me that the others were just dragging us down. Apparently the crew's massive debt was actually Seth's debt to the others, as he borrowed a lot of cash from Voldarris and a lot of sex with Evensga. Figures.

I laid on the couch in the living block for a bit feeling even more lonely, as it was just going to be the two of us for a while. The llamanians are still after my race, so I feel a little less safe in the care of just one person. However, Seth just told me that he is proud of me for saving him. Given how much he loves me, he decided to change the name of the Pelvis Crushers to the Vortox Pummelers in a weird, twisted homage. The "Vortox" part is fine, but the "Pummelers" is a bit on the nose given that the last crew died to "Pummel"-Spears.

Seth also told me that I was going to be learning how to pilot the ship now, given that he cannot legally drive a GU-tagged ship for some reason. Guess I'm going to be a pilot then, as Seth tends to want to go to a lot of places.

I think I'm going to finally finish this multi-entry as it is late and I cannot think of anything else to write. I will be back next week, I guess.

-Fet
